1. Stock Trading Platform → CP (Consistency + Partition Tolerance)

Why?
Accuracy is critical (no wrong trades or balances)
System must remain consistent even during network issues
Availability can be slightly sacrificed

Example: You’d rather delay a trade than execute a wrong one

2. Content Delivery Network (CDN) → AP (Availability + Partition Tolerance)

Why?
Content (images, videos) must always be available
Slight inconsistency (old version of content) is acceptable
Works globally with distributed servers

Example: Seeing a slightly outdated image is okay

3. Airline Booking System → CP (Consistency + Partition Tolerance)

Why?
Must avoid double booking of seats
Data consistency is more important than availability
System may block requests during failures

Example: Better to show “try again later” than overbook seats

4. Video Streaming Service → AP (Availability + Partition Tolerance)

Why?
Continuous streaming is priority
Minor inconsistencies (buffering, quality variation) are acceptable
Needs high scalability and uptime

Example: Video keeps playing even if quality drops
